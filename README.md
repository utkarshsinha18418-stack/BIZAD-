# BIZAD-

Conducted a comprehensive **competitor and market analysis** of professional networking, community, learning, and event-management platforms including **LinkedIn, Glassdoor, Bluelearn, Eventbeep, and Circle**. Evaluated competitors based on their key features, strengths, weaknesses, USPs, networking capabilities, community engagement, and user experience.

Analyzed emerging trends in the professional social media space, including **AI-driven innovation, B2B influencer growth, social commerce, employee advocacy, remote work, and personalized networking**. Identified major market opportunities and threats such as growing demand for professional networking, skill development, increasing competition, privacy concerns, and changing user preferences.

Developed a **Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy** using TAM, SAM, and SOM analysis, followed by market segmentation across corporate professionals, freelancers, job seekers, entrepreneurs, startups, and industry experts. Created user personas based on their goals, challenges, and preferred platform features.
Designed a **freemium and tiered pricing strategy** with Basic, Professional, and Enterprise plans, along with potential transaction and advertising revenue streams. Finally, developed positioning and value propositions focused on career growth, networking, professional visibility, entrepreneurship, and expert collaboration.
